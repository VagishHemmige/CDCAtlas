# CDCAtlas: Download 'CDC AtlasPlus' Data and Estimate Tract-Level Counts

Downloads HIV, sexually transmitted infection, tuberculosis and viral
hepatitis surveillance data from 'AtlasPlus', the interactive tool of
the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), through the
endpoints its web application uses. Estimates suppressed county counts,
reports the difference between state totals and the county counts
supplied, and allocates county counts to census tracts or block groups
in proportion to American Community Survey (ACS) populations. Not
affiliated with or endorsed by CDC.
